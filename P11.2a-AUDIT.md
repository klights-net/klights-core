`src/bootstrap/composition_adapters/` after P11.2a. “Calls” counts external
port calls in the method body; branches count semantic decision branches, not
ordinary `Result` mapping. Test-only constructors and hooks are excluded.

Removed root policy owners: node metrics (`klights-kubelet`), worker TLS/CSR
(`klights-auth`), freshness/resource query/projected snapshot/watch scope
(`klights-watch`), and leader routing snapshot policy (`klights-networking`).

Owner tests: `klights-watch` positioned-session/recovery tests cover scope and
snapshot expiry; `klights-networking::service_routing::leader_state_source`
covers all six leader-fresh lists; leader API/RPC contracts cover typed watch
scope and JSON/protobuf/RPC propagation. The isolated external-engine graph
fixture (`tests/fixtures/fake_external_engine_graph`) compiles without a scope
resolver and its `second_external_client_commit_wakes_a_local_positioned_watch_session`
test verifies cross-instance positioned-watch wakeup under the typed request
scope contract. Permanent execution-boundary ownership follows the moved
worker credential and TLS implementations in `klights-auth`; the Phase 14
auth ownership inventory names the canonical worker CSR client path. The
affected Redb/SQLite certification suite keeps its process-global SQLite
historical-window pause instrumentation serialized across its two pause-hook
scenarios, so the selector’s parallel package run remains deterministic.

## Exact inventory reconciliation

The original Q11.2a glob contained 31 files. The retained 26 are
`api_state_adapter`, `authority_adapter`, `bound_pod_finalization_adapter`,
`cluster_store_replication_adapter`, `cni_socket_adapter`,
`committed_outbox_delivery_adapter`, `custom_resource_read_adapter`,
`generated_handler_adapter`, `leader_bootstrap_store_adapter`,
`leader_maintenance_adapter`, `leader_topology_cleanup_adapter`, `mod`,
`node_admin_adapter`, `node_log_runtime_adapter`, `node_routing_metadata`,
`outbox_payload_codec_adapter`, `outbox_response_codec_adapter`,
`pod_event_adapter`, `pod_native_adapter`, `pod_repository_persistence_adapter`,
`positioned_watch_adapter`, `projected_token_resource_adapter`,
`remote_informer_cache_adapter`, `resource_admission_adapter`,
`signing_key_state_adapter`, and `watch_stream_adapter`.

The removed five are `node_metrics_adapter` → `klights-kubelet`,
`worker_credential_store_adapter` and `worker_csr_http_adapter` →
`klights-auth`, `resource_query_adapter` → `klights-watch`, and
`networking_state_adapter` → `klights-networking`. Thus 26 retained + 5
removed covers exactly 31, with no duplicate or gap.
